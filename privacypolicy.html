Privacy Policy

GSport respects your privacy. Our app requests access to your device’s camera solely for internal augmented reality (AR) detection purposes.

We do not store, share, or transmit any images or video captured by your camera. All camera data is processed locally on your device and used exclusively to enable AR functionality within the app.

By using GSport, you consent to this limited use of your device’s camera.

For any questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at radim.kliment@cvut.cz.
