Privacy Policy

GSport respects your privacy. Our app requests access to your device’s camera solely for internal augmented reality (AR) detection purposes.

We do not store, share, or transmit any images or video captured by your camera. All camera data is processed locally on your device and used exclusively to enable AR functionality within the app.

By using GSport, you consent to this limited use of your device’s camera.

For any questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at radim.kliment@cvut.cz.

Account Deletion

If you have an account with GSport, you may request that your account and associated data be deleted at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and data, please contact us at:

radim.kliment@cvut.cz

What Data is Deleted

  • Account information (e.g., email and profile data)
  • Any app usage data associated with your account

What Data is Retained

We may retain certain information only where legally required (e.g., financial transaction records) and only for the minimum required retention period.

Once your deletion request is completed, your account will be permanently removed and cannot be restored.
